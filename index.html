    How to play

    Each day brings five prompts — one per category, drawn from the canon of real quiz bowl: geography, history, literature, science, fine arts, with the occasional turn through myth and philosophy.

    You may give up to five answers per prompt, or stop at any time.

    Three right and two wrong nets zero. Knowing when to stop is the game. Eighty points is a perfect day.

    Half an answer is neither. Say “Henry” when the answer is Henry VII and you will be asked to be more specific — it costs you nothing and does not use up an answer.

    Spelling is forgiving, accents and leading articles are ignored, and common alternate titles are accepted.
